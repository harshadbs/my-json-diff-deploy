How to use

  1. Paste a Unix timestamp in seconds or milliseconds to see it as a date in UTC and your local timezone.
  2. Paste any date string or pick a date to get its timestamp.
  3. Use the live clock at the top to copy the current time.

Working with epoch time

Epoch timestamps avoid timezone and daylight-saving ambiguity, which makes them the standard for logs, databases, JWT claims and APIs. This converter shows every representation you might need next to each other, with one-click copy, and highlights the relative time so you can spot stale or future values instantly.
