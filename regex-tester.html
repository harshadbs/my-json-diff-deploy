How to use

  1. Type a pattern between the slashes and toggle flags such as g (all matches) or i (ignore case).
  2. Paste the text to test. Matches are highlighted and listed with their capture groups.
  3. Open Replace to preview a replacement using $1 or $<name> references.

Testing JavaScript regular expressions

The tester runs your pattern with the browser's RegExp engine, so what works here works in your JavaScript and TypeScript code. Start from a common pattern, refine it while watching the highlights update, then copy the pattern and flags into your project.
